## Context

### The Dispute
NHS resident doctors (formerly "junior doctors") are in dispute with the UK government over:
1. **Pay restoration** - Real pay is ~20% below 2008 levels
2. **Training places** - Shortage of specialty training positions
3. **Exam fees** - Mandatory royal college examination fees
4. **LED contracts** - Protection for locally employed doctors

### Key Players

#### Government Side
- **Prime Minister**: Keir Starmer (Labour, since July 2024)
- **Health Secretary**: James Murray (appointed May 14, 2026, ex-Treasury)
- **Previous Health Secretary**: Wes Streeting (resigned May 2026)
- **Treasury**: Controls the budget, limits spending
- **DDRB**: Independent body recommending 3.5% pay increase (March 2026)

#### Union Side
- **BMA**: British Medical Association (union)
- **RDC**: Resident Doctors Committee (strike committee)
- **RDC Chair**: Dr. Jack Fletcher
- **Eligible voters**: ~54,400 BMA members
- **Total resident doctors**: ~81,000 in England

### Current Situation (May 27, 2026)

✅ **Strike announced**: June 15-19, 2026 (4 days, 07:00 Mon → 06:59 Fri)  
✅ **Mandate valid**: Until August 2026 (from re-ballot: 93% yes, 52.5% turnout)  
❌ **March offer rejected**: Avg 4.9%, FY1 +6.2%, FY2 +7.1%  
🔥 **Accelerated places lost**: 1,000 places already "burnt" due to strike timing  
💰 **New minister stance**: Murray refuses to increase rejected offer  
📊 **Pay gap**: Real pay ~20% below 2008 levels

## Playable Organs (6 Teams)

### 1. RDC - Resident Doctors Committee
**Leader**: Dr. Jack Fletcher  
**Role**: Announces strikes, negotiates, recommends referendum outcome  
**Goals**:
- Deal accepted by base with pay+jobs (50%)
- Not below DDRB / not "sellout" (20%)
- Places and fees actually implemented (20%)
- Maintain mandate and unity (10%)

**Red Lines**:
- Don't recommend offer without concrete places and pay restoration progress
- Don't sell out the base for vague promises

**Levers**: Strike calendar, referendum recommendation, public pressure, grade work

## Victory Conditions

Victory is determined by completing organ-specific goals, not just reaching a deal.

**Scoring**:
- **≥ 70%** of goal weights completed = **Victory** ✅
- **40-69%** = **Partial success** ⚠️
- **< 40%** = **Defeat** ❌ (even if deal accepted)

**Common failure**: If no referendum acceptance by July 5, 2026 → July strikes and political deadlock. All organs fail main goal except those whose main goal is conflict escalation.

## Legal Strike Requirements

For a legal strike in UK healthcare:

1. **Postal ballot** of union members
   - Turnout ≥ 50% required
   - For "important public services" (healthcare): additionally ≥ 40% of ALL eligible members must vote "yes"

2. **14 days notice** before strike action

3. **Mandate valid for 6 months** (extendable to 9 by agreement)
   - After expiry: need re-ballot

4. **Patient safety** during strike
   - Hospitals agree on minimum emergency coverage (derogation)
   - Strike affects elective appointments, not emergency care

5. **Any offer goes to member referendum**
   - Simple majority of those voting decides
   - RDC can recommend "accept" but base may disagree

## Three "Locks" in Any Deal

Every deal must pass through three locks:

### 1. Strike Lock
- Mandate validity
- Notice periods
- Calendar constraints
- Cost per day (waiting list, locum expenses, public support, burnt places)

### 2. Ratification Lock
- Member referendum
- Simple majority required
- RDC must "sell" deal to divided base
- Grade split matters (FY1/FY2 vs ST vs LED have different interests)

### 3. Fiscal Anchor
- DDRB recommendation: 3.5%
- Treasury limit
- Going beyond limit triggers demands from other public sector workers
- Trade-offs: not just money, but places, fees, pay structure

## Hidden Metrics (Not Visible to Players)

These metrics exist and affect outcomes but players don't see exact values:

### Grade Split
- FY1/FY2 support for offer
- ST support for offer
- LED support for offer
- Values 0-1: higher = more support in that grade

### Treasury Hardness (0-10)
- 0 = willing to give any money
- 10 = categorical no to any limit breach
- Start: 8 (Murray is financier, bound by instructions)

### Consultant Contagion (0-10)
- Risk that resident deal triggers consultant demands
- High value increases HMT pressure on DHSC

### Media Heat (0-10)
- Media attention intensity
- High heat amplifies effect of public statements and leaks
- Start: 7 (minister change, strike, waiting list)

## Glossary (Key Terms)

- **NHS**: National Health Service - state healthcare system in England, free at point of use
- **Trust**: Organization managing hospital(s)
- **Resident doctor**: Doctor in training (former junior doctor): FY1/FY2 + ST
- **FY1/FY2**: First/second year of foundation programme (internship)
- **ST1-ST8**: Specialty training stages (residency)
- **Consultant**: Senior doctor, final career stage
- **LED**: Locally employed doctor - outside training programme
- **Locum**: Temporary replacement doctor (expensive)
- **Waiting list**: Queue for elective treatment
- **BMA**: British Medical Association - doctors' union
- **RDC**: Resident Doctors Committee - BMA strike committee
- **DHSC**: Department of Health and Social Care
- **HMT/Treasury**: HM Treasury - finance ministry
- **Chancellor**: Finance minister (not German Chancellor!)
- **Downing Street**: Prime Minister's office
- **DDRB**: Independent body recommending doctor pay increases
- **Pay restoration**: Restoring real pay to 2008 levels
- **Nodal points**: Pay steps linked to competencies; way to give more without breaking base rate
- **Ballot/re-ballot**: Postal vote for strike mandate / repeat
- **Mandate**: Legal right to strike (6 months)
- **Referendum**: Member vote on offer (simple majority)
- **Picket line**: Protest outside hospital during strike
- **Derogation**: Agreed minimum emergency coverage during strike
- **Royal colleges**: Professional colleges setting exams and standards
- **AoMRC**: Academy of Medical Royal Colleges
